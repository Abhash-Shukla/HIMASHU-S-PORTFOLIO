ABOUT ME

CRAFTING STORIES FRAME BY FRAME

I’m Himanshu Kumar, a freelance video editor based in Noida, India. I’ve spent more than 2 years refining the art of post-production across YouTube, documentary, commercial, and social content — working with creators, brands, and agencies who refuse to settle for average.

My philosophy is simple: every cut is intentional, every transition serves the story, and every second of silence is earned. I don’t just assemble footage — I architect emotion.
